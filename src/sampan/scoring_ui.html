About these topic scores

Each score adds five checks worth up to 2 points each. Source-backed meanings, headings, detail, focus and links provide signals; human review is still needed. With Kimi enabled, AI proposes search phrases and the server checks supporting quotations against the supplied site text. Kimi also identifies equivalent or related meanings in source titles, headings, passages and link labels. The server validates the referenced text and calculates the points: equivalent wording receives full matching credit; related wording receives partial credit. Model judgments still need review. Local mode extracts phrases directly from site content. Repeated and overlapping phrases are filtered; results vary by website. Search demand, competition and advertising cost need external keyword data. A project or research mention does not establish a current service.

      How the scores work and what is not measured

      Each score has a separate 10-point weighted rubric. Pass earns full credit, partial earns the stated fraction, and a confirmed failure earns none. Verified readiness divides earned credit by all applicable weight. Unresolved checks earn no verified credit and remain visible as uncertainty, not failures; N/A is excluded. The ceiling assumes every unresolved check passes. No evidence produces no score. Category grades use the same method. Editorial conventions and optional structures can require review without being failures. High scores do not establish Google indexing or AI citations.

        Clear expectations, from the start.

        Does a high score guarantee visibility?

        No. Readiness, keyword demand, Google performance and AI citations are different measurements. We report unavailable outcome data as not measured.

        Will SAMPAN rewrite the website every week?

        No. Changes should follow evidence and the owner's approval. Monitoring, content updates and publishing are separate parts of the workflow.

        Is Search Console connected here?

        Not yet. A dated export or authorized read-only connection is the next measurement step for the pilot.
