Before the managed pilot starts

Prepare the evidence, access and working agreement.

01

Confirm the scope and owner

Record the owner, approved domains, priority services or features, target audience, countries, languages and permitted collection. Approve the service scope, reporting frequency and responsibilities.

02

Connect real search feedback

Keep the client as Search Console owner. Start with an authorized read-only connection or dated performance export. Record missing data honestly. Add consent-appropriate analytics and agree what counts as a useful enquiry or interaction.

03

Agree on the targets

Review nine search queries and ten AI questions for each pilot, with country, language, page and observation date. Separate branded discovery from service or feature intent. Query suggestions are not measured search demand.

04

Choose one focused improvement

Use an evidence-backed change brief with expected effect and an acceptance check. Confirm factual claims. For VX Real, distinguish current services from past work; for marksix.gold, preserve its independence and no-prediction limitations.

05

Prepare release and measurement

Inspect each site's existing CMS or deployment route, backups, forms and tracking. Agree who approves and publishes, retain a rollback version, then compare fixed targets over suitable periods. A weekly review does not require a weekly release.

Planned next: reviewed action plans, release records and outcome comparison. Public client accounts, automated publication and billing are separate launch decisions.
