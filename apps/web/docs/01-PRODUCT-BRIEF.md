# Product brief and requirements — SAMPAN.ai corporate site

## Why rebuild the public site?

SAMPAN.ai is moving from a standalone GEO/SEO-tool positioning to a broader **enterprise AI presence and transformation** offering, with **AI Corporate Ambassador** as the tangible, understandable entry product. The existing SEO/GEO prototype must remain intact; public marketing changes do not imply its completion or the release of other services.

## Intended users and purchasing motivations

- **SME owner / managing director:** wants a visible branded AI spokesperson on their website; wants to show the company is ready for the AI era.
- **Marketing director:** wants on-brand answers, product explanations and reusable company presenter videos.
- **Customer service manager:** wants fewer basic enquiries and clear transfer of complex questions to staff.
- **Website or marketing agency / channel partner:** wants a white-labelable implementation service for existing clients.
- **Technical/security reviewer:** wants clear privacy, deployment, data boundaries, vendor dependencies and limitations.

## Core message hierarchy

1. **A digital face for the company**, not a generic chatbot.
2. **Company-trained, approved answers** for frequently asked questions and services.
3. **One reusable spokesperson** for live website interaction and future marketing video creation.
4. **Cantonese-first Hong Kong service**, pending real voice/performance tests.
5. **Secure and manageable**: customer controls content, permissions and deployment; never claim certifications not earned.
6. **Broader services** only after presenting the flagship, without confusing buyers.

## Desired conversion actions

Primary: **Request a Demo / 預約示範**.
Secondary: **Explore the Digital Ambassador / 認識 AI 企業代言人**, **Explore Solutions / 了解企業 AI 方案**.
Admin preview: **Try Demo Workspace / 體驗管理平台**.
Contact actions should be either a working validated route or clearly labelled prototype; no fake submitted-success acknowledgements.

## Out of scope tonight

No production avatar rendering, real-time voice AI, RAG ingestion, authenticated tenant administration, actual WhatsApp orders, payments, real video rendering, cloud provisioning, source-code duplication from D-ID, client document intake or externally published customer data. Make the marketing site look credible while representing capability status accurately.
