# SAMPAN.ai — project background and direction

We want to build a Semrush-inspired service for SMEs that combines traditional search optimization (SEO) with visibility in AI-generated answers (GEO). Our aim is to help businesses attract relevant customers and generate qualified enquiries, then turn successful improvements into a repeatable managed service under SAMPAN.ai.

Use **ESGtrace.net as the main service-development pilot** and **VXReal.com as the existing reference and comparison site**. Start by understanding each product and its audience. Use AI to explore opportunities, challenge the current approach and propose priorities and success measures. The team has flexibility to shape the approach.

## What we want to learn

Does the checker identify useful opportunities accurately? Do its topic suggestions match the customer's actual business? Can we translate findings into changes that produce measurable results? What should be automated, and what needs human review?

Track three different things: **readiness**, **observed Google performance**, and **observed AI visibility**. Search Console supports impressions, clicks, CTR and average position; GA4 supports traffic and enquiry measurement when configured; a repeatable AI-answer benchmark supports mentions, citations and factual accuracy. Their access and implementation are separate tasks. None of these is a guaranteed first-page ranking or citation probability.

Please return a short findings summary, the first few recommended improvements and a practical measurement approach. Capture the lessons so we can reuse them for future SME clients. Agree specific publication, account or hosting changes with Barry before making them.
