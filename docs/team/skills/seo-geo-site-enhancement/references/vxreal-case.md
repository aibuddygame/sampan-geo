# VX Real pilot: what to reuse

Evidence refreshed from the selected local upload source and SAMPAN progress records on 2026-10-31. This is a dated implementation case, not a fresh production audit or evidence of improved search traffic.

## Retained source improvements

The selected `VXReal-Cloudflare-Upload-2026 Oct` folder has 73 files: 11 content pages plus a 404, assets and control/reference files.

| Area | Work retained in the source | Purpose and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crawl/discovery | robots.txt with public crawl policy and /api/ exclusion; sitemap.xml; hosting content-type rules | Discover intended public pages and deliver usable policy/XML. Does not guarantee indexing. |
| URL/indexing signals | Page-specific titles/descriptions, canonicals, old Solutions redirects, non-indexed 404 | Reduce ambiguity/duplicates and preserve old routes; search may choose different titles/canonicals. |
| Structured identity | Organization/WebSite/WebPage records; Service records on Solutions; synchronized company.jsonld | Expose consistent facts matching visible text; no special AI schema or rich-result guarantee. |
| Reference directory | Optional llms.txt links to public pages and company profile | A maintained pointer, not proof that AI providers read it. No mandatory knowledge-base platform. |
| Relevant links | Three specific case links on Solutions and eight related-service links on project pages | Connect the current offer with relevant work without pretending all past projects are current products. |
| Factual market coverage | Owner-confirmed Hong Kong, Singapore and Southeast Asia coverage; English service language in footer and business records | Actual availability, not local offices or localized search performance. No separate regional pages/hreflang in preferred revision. |
| Media/presentation | Favicons with page references; intrinsic sizes on 63 image elements; async decoding and selective below-fold lazy loading; narrow CSS refinements | Support recognizable branding and layout stability. Search favicon display/field speed not established by local markup. |
| Enquiry measurement | Base Google tag on 12 HTML pages, once per page; supplied lead helper on Solutions after base tag; event after accepted /api/contact response | Separate paid-campaign measurement. This does not itself improve organic rankings/GEO. No personal form fields added to event. |

## Important revision decision

An expanded draft added a Markets page, FAQs and longer service/project sections. The owner preferred the original concise presentation. The later L015 revision restored that style and retained targeted links, market facts and media refinements. Do not list the discarded Markets/FAQ expansion as the current deliverable or copy it into every future client site.

The same-page enquiry form and JSON contract remained intact. Eleven offline conversion scenarios passed on 2026-10-28; the matching 73-file ZIP was hash-verified. Earlier L015 checks covered 12 pages and 249 local URL/fragment references. These checks prove those dated local artifacts, not current production/email delivery/Ads attribution.

## Known limits

The owner previously reported uploading improvements; retained L003/L012 scans verified selected live resources/pages on 2026-10-26. Current production parity with every subsequent local revision was not rechecked for this skill. The historical 9.1 SEO / 6.3 GEO primary-page readiness report used an earlier rubric/capture: it is not an outcome uplift or a current whole-site score. Actual indexing, country rankings, keyword demand, AI citations, traffic uplift and conversion attribution require separate evidence.

SAMPAN Traditional Chinese/mixed-language processing later passed synthetic/browser tests, but that is a tool capability experiment, not a Chinese translation of VX Real or proof of market accuracy. Do not promise the scorer's heuristics are fully calibrated; provider-association/link-support biases remain a separate backlog.

## Reusable lessons

Start from the latest client source and current facts. Preserve design and contact/backend behavior. Keep visible and structured information consistent. Use small relevant additions and optional reference files. Validate a reviewable handoff before deployment, and measure real search/AI/business outcomes separately.
